Scoring, in the open

Seven floors of the Honors Living-Learning Community compete all year. Every rule and every point value is published here, so anyone can check the maths.

The Categories

Where points come from

Two kinds of event, scored differently on purpose. Where every floor is invited, each resident who signs in is worth a point. Where only your own floor is invited, a raw headcount would be meaningless — so those categories are judged on a percentage of your roster, or on turnout thresholds.

The Fine Print

Counting rules

    Why Two Systems

    Shared events vs floor events

    At a shared HLLC event every floor is invited, so a headcount is a fair contest and each resident who signs in is worth a point.

    At a floor event nobody else is invited, so counting heads would just reward whoever schedules the most. Floor meetings and iConvos are therefore ranked on the percentage of your own roster that takes part — which also means a floor of 48 is never at a disadvantage against a floor of 52. An RA's own programming clears turnout thresholds instead, capped at 30 points, so effort is rewarded but volume alone cannot win a season.

    Where the numbers come from

    Attendance and floor-meeting turnout are counted from Roompact sign-ins. Community initiatives, notes for introduction and resident nominations are recorded by the Honors LLC staff. Cleanliness is named each week by the building service workers.

    Nothing on this site is a stored total. Every score, rank and percentage is added up fresh from the list of individual awards each time the page loads, so the standings and the activity log can never disagree with each other.
