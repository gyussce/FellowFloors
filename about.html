Counting rules

    Why Attendance Is Bundled

    Keeping it proportionate

    There were twenty-two HLLC events between move-in and the first week of classes. Scoring each one separately at 10 / 6 / 2 would have put up to 190 points on attendance alone, against roughly 60 for every other category combined across the whole semester. Attendance would have become the competition.

    So a run of events is added up into a single turnout figure and placed once. Same data, same winner, proportionate weight. Turnout is measured as check-ins divided by roster size, so a floor of 48 is not punished for being smaller than a floor of 52.

    Where the numbers come from

    Attendance and floor-meeting turnout are counted from Roompact sign-ins. Community initiatives, notes for introduction and resident nominations are recorded by the Honors LLC staff. Cleanliness is named each week by the building service workers.

    Nothing on this site is a stored total. Every score, rank and percentage is added up fresh from the list of individual awards each time the page loads, so the standings and the activity log can never disagree with each other.
