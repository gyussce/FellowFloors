How points work

There are two kinds of event here, and they are scored differently on purpose. Knowing which is which explains everything on the board below — including why a floor can turn out well and still show a zero in a category.

🌟

Everybody invited

Shared HLLC events

Every resident of your floor who signs in is one point. No cap, no placement, nobody to beat — turn out and you score. This is where most points on the board have come from so far, and it is the one category you can move tonight.

🏠

Your floor only

Floor events

Nobody else is invited, so a raw headcount would be unfair. Floor meetings and iConvos are ranked on the percentage of your floor that takes part — top three score. Your RA's own events earn points by clearing turnout thresholds instead, so a floor cannot win the year by running more events than everyone else.
